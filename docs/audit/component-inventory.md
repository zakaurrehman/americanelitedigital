## Reuse vs duplication

- The **contact form** exists as two Forminator forms (IDs 1061 and 1065) placed about 20 times across pages.
- The **CTA band**, **page banner** and **service card** are rebuilt per page, which is why copy drifted (the "Brands Design" residue and the wrong card text).
- The **header and footer** are the only true shared templates (Header Footer Elementor).

The redesign replaces these with a small set of typed React components fed from a single content layer (`src/content/`). Copy changes in one place and stays consistent across pages.
